This PR guards calls to System.setProperty to avoid a NullPointerException during startup and adds optional cookie-seeding from the user's `%APPDATA%\\ctbrec` cookie files to aid debugging and testing of logged-in sessions.

Changes:
- `ctbrec.io.HttpClient`: added `safeSetProperty(...)` to guard System.setProperty calls and prevent NPEs during startup.
- Cookie loader: optional loader that seeds OkHttp `CookieJar` from `cookies-*.json` files in `%APPDATA%\\ctbrec` to allow running the app with previously-authenticated sessions for debugging.

Build notes:
- I ran `mvn -DskipTests package` locally; compilation started but failed in this environment due to missing JavaFX classes (this environment is headless and lacks JavaFX on the compile classpath). See attached `build.log` for details.

Testing steps for maintainers:
1. Pull this branch and build with a JDK that includes JavaFX (or add OpenJFX to the Maven config): `mvn -DskipTests package`.
2. Run the packaged app or JAR and confirm no NPE appears during startup and that the app can load cookies when `%APPDATA%\\ctbrec` contains `cookies-*.json` files.

Related: packaged-distribution patch and PR: https://github.com/SpoiledPanda/ctbrec/pull/3
